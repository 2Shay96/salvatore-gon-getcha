SALVATORE GANACCI v1.1
Author: 2Shay. Release prepared 25 October 2026.

REQUIREMENTS
Fallout: New Vegas Steam 1.4.0.525, FalloutNV.esm, and xNVSE 6.4.9 or newer
6.x. Launch through your working xNVSE/4GB setup. No DLC, ShowOff, JIP,
JohnnyGuitar or GECK MCP companion is required. xNVSE is not bundled.
The SalvatoreGanacci.dll included in NVSE/Plugins is required.

INSTALL / UPDATE
Close FNV. Back up your saves and retain the previous mod archive.
Install this complete ZIP with Vortex, replacing earlier Salvatore versions.
Enable SalvatoreGanacci.esp and let this package win its asset conflicts.
Disable development probe/filming plugins for ordinary play. Restart FNV
through xNVSE. Do not combine several Salvatore packages as separate versions.
This ZIP uses the Data-root layout; manual installation uses the same paths.
No automatic removal of existing duplicate items or save cleanup is performed.

MAIN EXPERIENCE
On a fresh save: "Salvatore Ganacci is watching. Be kind to animals.
This is not a suggestion."
The first qualifying player attack on a supported peaceful animal plays
the eagle warning and starts one 1-3 game-day countdown. Further attacks
do not restart the timer or repeat the screech. Arrival waits for outdoors
in the main Mojave. Standard Brahmin and selected peaceful Goodsprings /
Jacobstown Bighorner bases are supported. Accidents count; companion hits,
mod-added species and universal trap/explosion coverage are not supported.
There is one natural encounter per save; owning Salvatore prevents another.

Far away, he approaches on the shoe. Its 37 frames loop forward/backward
at 24 fps over three seconds, without repeated endpoints. At about 10m,
he switches once to his regular sprites. Idle and dancing also ping-pong.
Fixed-size alpha-tested clip selection avoids scale easing and the observed
idle/dancing visibility gap. The current visual appearance is owner accepted.

Defeat and collect him. With exactly one Salvatore carried, LEFT-CLICK his
Weapons entry normally, acknowledge the popup and close Pip-Boy to deploy.
Your selected gun, exact inventory copy, condition and attachments are left
alone. Activate the companion to store him. Actual repaired Salvatore
condition is read from the clicked item; use ordinary weapon/merchant repair.
Duplicates and condition below 1% refuse deployment without consumption.
Drop is an ordinary inventory action. Hotkey/console equipping Salvatore
is outside the supported deployment interface.

OPTIONAL REMOTE / RECOVERY
New saves receive no automatic Remote; existing copies remain available.
The optional Aid Remote and state-aware fetch quest remain console-accessible.
Run GetModIndex "SalvatoreGanacci.esp" in this game session; use its two-digit
hexadecimal index in place of XX below. Never type XX literally or reuse an
old prefix after changing load order.
  player.additem XX00082F 1   Optional Ganacci Remote
  startquest XX000818        Retrieve existing representation; carried is a no-op
  sqv XX00080F               Inspect main quest
Do not use placeatme/additem to create replacement actors/items for recovery.
Reset is not condition repair or automatic duplicate cleanup.

UPGRADES / LIMITS
Plugin filename, FormIDs and prior script variable positions are retained.
NativeVersion/NativeDeploy are appended. Old tracker records remain but their
quests are stopped. Existing v1.0 countdowns and initialized states retain
their saved progression; an upgrade need not replay a fresh-install message.
Keep original saves: broad v1.0 upgrade combinations are not fully tested.
Existing broken duplicate saves require conservative manual recovery.

Owner acceptance includes normal-click deploy/store/redeploy, merchant repair,
carried/deployed save/reload, fists, Mercenary grenade launcher, duplicate
laser-pistol copies, a suppressor-bearing sniper, a retextured .357, duplicate
Salvatore refusal, broken-item refusal, visual follow-up and shoe loop/10m switch.
This is not a guarantee for every mod-added weapon or competing inventory hook.
New-form weapon mods and interrupted-request edge cases remain untested in-game;
nonzero condition below 1% has host-side checks, not a separate live test.
Animal detection overrides 23 vanilla creature script bindings and may conflict
with creature overhauls. No universal repair-bag compatibility is claimed.
Broader terrain/obstacle, far-phase defeat, travel/audio and upgrade cases retain
their existing test limits. Optional companion-only distribution is deferred.

VATS KNOWN ISSUE
Only Torso is offered as a VATS target; inherited robot limbs, sensors and
combat inhibitor are removed for Salvatore only. VATS currently reports 0%
hit chance against Salvatore with guns and unarmed attacks. This is a known
unresolved bug: torso selection does not mean VATS attacks work. Fight him
outside VATS. Normal NPC VATS was checked separately and works.

DIAGNOSTICS / ROLLBACK
Native log: game folder/SalvatoreGanacci-native.log (replaced at each launch).
A successful deployment logs CANCEL, COMMIT and DEPLOY_COMPLETE with sameGun=1.
Incompatible runtime or an existing conflicting hook is refused, not overwritten.
If the DLL cannot load, normal-click cancellation is unavailable.
For rollback, close FNV, restore the complete previous package through your mod
manager, and load an untouched compatible backup save. Prefer a pre-mod save
for uninstall; do not remove unrelated shared assets.
