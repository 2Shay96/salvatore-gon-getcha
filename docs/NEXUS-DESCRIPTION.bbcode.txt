[center][size=6][color=#E6B85C][b]SALVATORE GON’ GETCHA[/b][/color][/size]
[size=4][color=#EAD8B0]He finds you. He fights you. Then he's your besty.[/color][/size][/center]

[color=#EAD8B0]Bring a dancing Salvatore Ganacci billboard sprite into Fallout: New Vegas. He starts as a hostile encounter in the Mojave; defeat him, collect him, and deploy him as a companion who follows, fights and explores nearby while you stand still.

This sprite mod was made with [url=https://github.com/2Shay96/sprite-forge]Sprite Forge Studio[/url], with the gameplay plugin authored in GECK. Sprite Forge Studio is a creation tool; you do not need it installed to play this mod.[/color]

[size=4][color=#E6B85C][b]FROM BOSS TO POCKET COMPANION[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Harming a supported peaceful animal starts a 1-3 in-game day countdown, warning and eagle screech. He arrives outdoors in the main Mojave; repeat hits do not restart the timer.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]VATS works against him: target his Torso with guns or fists for a normal hit chance.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Separate main, idle and attack sprites; main, idle and shoe use forward/backward loops at 24 fps. The shoe approach switches to regular sprites at about 10 metres.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]A constant spatial theme while he is active, which pauses in menus and the Pip-Boy and carries on where it left off, an occasional attack quip, and two randomly selected idle clips.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Defeat and collect him, then left-click Salvatore in Weapons and close Pip-Boy to deploy. Your selected weapon, exact inventory copy, condition and attachments are left alone.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]He follows, fights enemies and roams nearby when you stop moving.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Companion defeats reduce his condition to approximately 66%, 33%, then 0%. Repair him before deploying again.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]The optional console-obtainable Ganacci Remote retains status and recovery controls. New saves no longer receive it automatically.[/color][/*]
[/list]


[size=4][color=#E6B85C][b]INSTALLATION & REQUIREMENTS[/b][/color][/size]
[color=#EAD8B0]v1.2 requires Fallout: New Vegas Steam 1.4.0.525, FalloutNV.esm and xNVSE 6.4.9 or newer 6.x. The required SalvatoreGanacci.dll is bundled. No DLC, ShowOff, JIP or GECK MCP is needed. Launch through your working xNVSE/4GB setup.

Close FNV, back up your saves, and install SalvatoreGanacci-v1.2.zip in place of older Salvatore packages. Enable SalvatoreGanacci.esp and restart through xNVSE. For manual installation, copy the plugin and meshes, textures and sound folders into Fallout New Vegas/Data, preserving their folder structure.

Use a save made before installing the mod for a fresh start. New saves do not receive the optional Remote; existing Remote copies remain. The first qualifying attack on a standard Brahmin or selected peaceful Goodsprings/Jacobstown Bighorner starts the encounter countdown. The natural encounter begins outdoors in the main Mojave worldspace; if you are indoors or on the Strip, head back into the Mojave.

After collecting Salvatore, left-click his Weapons entry, acknowledge the popup and close Pip-Boy to deploy. Activate him to store. Actual repaired condition is preserved. Duplicate items or condition below 1% refuse deployment without consumption; repair him normally before trying again. Drop is an ordinary inventory action.[/color]

[color=#EAD8B0]Showcase note: deathclaw footage uses an optional filming add-on with invulnerability and increased punch damage. That add-on is not included in the main download. The green scanline cover is promotional artwork; gameplay screenshots show the actual sprite appearance.[/color]

[size=4][color=#E6B85C][b]KNOWN ISSUES - v1.2[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]VATS offers only Torso, with inherited robot limbs/sensors/combat inhibitor removed. Fists get 0% where there is no navmesh path to him, as for any target. If he stays off-screen in VATS for over 2 seconds, that VATS session gives 0%; re-enter VATS with him in view.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Repair works through the normal item repair flow. Repair-bag compatibility has not been tested.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]His defeated-sprite loop still plays in menus. Scripted pickup can be silent, and a brief sprite flicker may occur on first appearance.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Back up saves before updating. Existing progression and test-mode settings are retained; old duplicate items are not automatically deleted. Broader old-save upgrades and newly added weapon forms are not universally tested.[/color][/*]
[/list]
[color=#EAD8B0]To uninstall, use your mod manager and preferably return to a save from before installation. For manual installs, remove only the files listed in this mod's archive and restart the game.[/color]
