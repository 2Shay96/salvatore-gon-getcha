[center][size=6][color=#E6B85C][b]SALVATORE GON’ GETCHA[/b][/color][/size]
[size=4][color=#EAD8B0]He finds you. He fights you. Then he's your besty.[/color][/size][/center]

[color=#EAD8B0]Ever wanted a dancing Salvatore Ganacci sprite hunting you across the Mojave? No? Too late.

He starts out as a boss fight. Beat him, pick him up, and from then on he's your dancing companion: he follows you, fights for you, and wanders around nearby whenever you stop moving.

I made him with [url=https://github.com/2Shay96/sprite-forge]Sprite Forge Studio[/url], my own tool for turning videos into game sprites. It's coming to Nexus soon (more on that below). The gameplay side was built in the GECK. You don't need Sprite Forge to play, just this mod.

v1.2 is the polish update, and honestly he feels polished af now: VATS finally works on him, and his music pauses when you open a menu, then picks up right where it left off.[/color]

[size=4][color=#E6B85C][b]FROM BOSS TO POCKET COMPANION[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Hurt a peaceful brahmin or bighorner and the clock starts. Within 1-3 in-game days you'll get a warning and an eagle screech... then he shows up, somewhere outdoors in the Mojave. Hitting more animals won't reset the timer.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]VATS works on him now. Aim for his torso with guns or fists for a normal hit chance.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]He's fully animated: his own main, idle and attack sprites, looping back and forth at 24 fps. On his way to you he does the shoe approach, then switches to his regular sprites at about 10 metres.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]He brings his own soundtrack. His theme plays around him while he's out, pauses whenever you're in a menu or the Pip-Boy, and carries on where it left off. Plus the odd attack quip and a couple of random idle clips.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Once you've beaten him and picked him up, left-click him in your Weapons and close the Pip-Boy to deploy him. It doesn't touch your current weapon, and he keeps his condition and attachments.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]He follows you, fights whatever's fighting you, and roams around when you stand still.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]He can go down too. Each time he's beaten as your companion, his condition drops: about 66%, then 33%, then 0%. Repair him before you send him out again.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]There's an optional Ganacci Remote (console only) with status and recovery controls. New saves don't get it automatically anymore.[/color][/*]
[/list]

[size=4][color=#E6B85C][b]INSTALL & REQUIREMENTS[/b][/color][/size]
[color=#EAD8B0][b]You'll need:[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Fallout: New Vegas, Steam version 1.4.0.525 (with FalloutNV.esm)[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]xNVSE 6.4.9 or newer (6.x)[/color][/*]
[/list]
[color=#EAD8B0]That's it. The Salvatore DLL is already in the zip, and you don't need any DLC, ShowOff, JIP or GECK MCP. Launch the game through xNVSE like you normally would (your 4GB setup is fine).[/color]

[color=#EAD8B0][b]To install:[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Close the game and back up your saves first.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Install SalvatoreGanacci-v1.2.zip with your mod manager. Got an older Salvatore version? Replace it, don't stack them.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Coming from v1.1? Same plugin, so your save carries on.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Make sure SalvatoreGanacci.esp is enabled, then launch through xNVSE.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Installing by hand? Drop the plugin plus the meshes, textures and sound folders into Fallout New Vegas/Data, keeping the folder structure.[/color][/*]
[/list]

[color=#EAD8B0][b]Getting him to show up:[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]For the cleanest start, use a save from before you installed the mod.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]The first hit on a regular brahmin, or one of the peaceful bighorners around Goodsprings or Jacobstown, starts the countdown.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]He only shows up outdoors in the main Mojave, so if you're inside or on the Strip, head back out.[/color][/*]
[/list]

[color=#EAD8B0][b]Deploying him:[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Left-click him in Weapons, hit OK on the popup and close the Pip-Boy.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Activate him to put him away. He keeps the condition you repaired him to.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]If he's under 1% condition (or you somehow have two of him), he won't come out, but you won't lose him either. Repair him and try again.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Dropping him works like dropping any other item.[/color][/*]
[/list]

[color=#EAD8B0][b]Quick heads-up:[/b] the deathclaw footage uses a separate filming add-on (invulnerability plus extra punch damage) that isn't in this download. And the green scanline cover is just promo art; the gameplay screenshots show what he actually looks like in-game.[/color]

[size=4][color=#E6B85C][b]KNOWN ISSUES (v1.2)[/b][/color][/size]
[color=#EAD8B0]Stuff I know about. Fixes will land when I get to them.[/color]
[list]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]In VATS you can only target his torso. Fists show 0% if you can't actually walk to him (same as any enemy). If he's been off-screen for over 2 seconds, VATS shows 0% for that go, so just back out and go in again with him in view.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Repairing him works the normal way. Repair bags haven't been tested yet.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]His defeated-sprite loop still plays in menus. Picking him up can sometimes be silent, and he might flicker for a sec the first time he appears.[/color][/*]
[*][color=#EAD8B0]Back up your saves before updating. Your progress and test settings carry over, but old duplicate items don't get cleaned up automatically, and I haven't tested every old save or weapon setup out there.[/color][/*]
[/list]
[color=#EAD8B0][b]Uninstalling:[/b] remove it with your mod manager, ideally loading a save from before you installed it. Installed by hand? Delete only the files from this mod's zip and restart the game.[/color]
