[center][size=6][color=#E6B85C][b]SALVATORE GON’ GETCHA[/b][/color][/size]
[size=4][color=#EAD8B0]He finds you. He fights you. Then he fits in your inventory.[/color][/size][/center]

[color=#EAD8B0]Bring a dancing Salvatore Ganacci billboard sprite into Fallout: New Vegas. He starts as a hostile encounter in the Mojave; defeat him, collect him, and deploy him as a companion who follows, fights and explores nearby while you stand still.

This sprite mod was made with [url=https://github.com/2Shay96/sprite-forge]Sprite Forge Studio[/url], with the gameplay plugin authored in GECK. Sprite Forge Studio is a creation tool; you do not need it installed to play this mod.[/color]

[size=4][color=#E6B85C][b]FROM BOSS TO POCKET COMPANION[/b][/color][/size]
[color=#EAD8B0][list]
[*]A hostile Salvatore encounter begins after 1-3 in-game days while outdoors in the Mojave.
[*]Separate main, idle and attack sprite animations, with smooth forward/backward main and idle loops at 24 fps.
[*]A constant spatial theme while he is active, an occasional attack quip, and two randomly selected idle clips.
[*]Defeat him to reveal a small collectible sprite, then equip the collected item to deploy your companion.
[*]He follows, fights enemies and roams nearby when you stop moving.
[*]Companion defeats reduce his condition to approximately 66%, 33%, then 0%. Repair him before deploying again.
[*]The Ganacci Remote lets you check his status, summon him nearby, send him away, reset the encounter and switch test mode.
[/list][/color]

[size=4][color=#E6B85C][b]INSTALLATION & REQUIREMENTS[/b][/color][/size]
[color=#EAD8B0]Requires Fallout: New Vegas and FalloutNV.esm. No DLC or NVSE required.

Install SalvatoreGanacci-v1.0.zip with your mod manager and enable SalvatoreGanacci.esp, then restart the game. For manual installation, copy the plugin and meshes, textures and sound folders into Fallout New Vegas/Data, preserving their folder structure.

Use a save made before installing the mod for a fresh start. The weightless Ganacci Remote appears in the Aid tab. Use it and close the Pip-Boy to open its menu. The natural encounter begins outdoors in the main Mojave worldspace; if you are indoors or on the Strip, head back into the Mojave.

After collecting Salvatore, equip the item to deploy him. Activate the companion to store him in your inventory. At 0% condition he is too battered to deploy: repair the item and equip it again.[/color]
